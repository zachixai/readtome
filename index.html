Read To Me – News Aloud: Support

Read To Me is an iPad app that reads the news and advice columns aloud for people who are blind or have low vision. One big button, then just talk.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: zachi@xai.health

Common questions

The app doesn't hear me. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition, and allow Read To Me. Speech recognition needs a real iPad.

How do I change what it reads or the voice? Press and hold Helper Settings (top-right) for about a second. A sighted helper can choose sections, news source, speed and voice there.

A story is only partly read. Some publications need the listener's own subscription. In Helper Settings, open that choice and sign in.

A more natural voice. Download Ava (Premium) in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English.
