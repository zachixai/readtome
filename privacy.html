Privacy Policy

Read To Me – News Aloud. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

Read To Me does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data. The developer operates no servers for this app and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app listens for your spoken answers (such as "yes", "no" or "stop"). Speech is converted to text with Apple's speech recognition, on the device when available. Apple may process audio under Apple's privacy policy when on-device recognition is unavailable. The developer never receives or stores your audio or transcripts.

Network use

The app connects directly from your iPad to news and column websites you choose (for example NPR, BBC, The New York Times, The Dallas Morning News, The Washington Post and uexpress.com, or an RSS feed address you enter) to fetch headlines and stories. Those sites may see standard connection information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Subscription sign-in details

If you choose to sign in to a newspaper subscription, the email and password are saved only in your iPad's Keychain and are sent only to that newspaper's own sign-in page. They are never sent to the developer.

Settings

Your choices (sections, voice, speed, news source) are stored only on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
